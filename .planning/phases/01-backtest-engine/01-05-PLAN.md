| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-1-08 | Repudiation | run_id collision | accept | run_id is MD5(symbol|tf|first|last|cost_hash)[:16] — sufficient for solo dev workflow; not a security identifier. |
